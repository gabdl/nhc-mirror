Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
1100 AM PDT Fri Sep 4 2026

For the eastern and central North Pacific east of 180 longitude:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Hurricane 
Karina, located well east of the Hawaiian Islands, on Hurricane 
Lowell, located several hundred miles south of the Hawaiian Islands, 
and on Hurricane Marie, located several hundred miles west- 
southwest of the southern tip of the Baja California Peninsula.

1. Offshore of Southwestern Mexico:
An area of low pressure may develop well offshore of southwestern 
Mexico early next week. Gradual development is possible, and a 
tropical depression could form around the middle of next week while 
it moves west-northwestward at 10 to 15 mph, likely remaining well 
offshore of Mexico.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...near 0 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...medium...50 percent.



Forecaster Adams/Evans
