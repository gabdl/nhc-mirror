Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS Central Pacific Hurricane Center Honolulu HI
Issued by NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
200 AM HST Thu Sep 17 2026

For the central North Pacific...between 140W and 180W:

1. Western East Pacific:
A tropical wave continues to produce disorganized showers and
thunderstorms well to the south-southwest of the southern tip of the
Baja California Peninsula. Environmental conditions appear
conducive for gradual development of this system, and a tropical
depression is likely to form this weekend or early next week as it
moves westward at 10 to 15 mph across the western part of the East
Pacific.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...20 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...70 percent.

2. Well East-Southeast of the Hawaiian Islands (Former TD15-E):
Showers and thunderstorms have increased somewhat with an area of
low pressure related to the remnants of Tropical Depression
Fifteen-E. Environmental conditions appear only marginally conducive
for some development of this system as it moves gradually westward,
remaining east-southeast of the Hawaiian Islands.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...10 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...10 percent.



Forecaster Papin

Para el Pacífico Norte central…entre 140 y 180 longitud oeste

Oeste del Pacífico Oriental: Una onda tropical continúa produciendo
aguaceros y tormentas desorganizadas hasta el sur-suroeste del

oeste del Pacífico este.

área de baja presión relacionada con los remanentes de la Depresión
Tropical Quince-E. Las condiciones ambientales parecen propiciar
solo marginalmente el desarrollo de este sistema, ya que se
desplazará gradualmente hacia el oeste, quedando en el este-sureste
de las islas hawaianas.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 48 horas...baja...10 por ciento.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 7 días...baja...10 por ciento.

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
800 AM EDT Thu Sep 17 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

1. Central Subtropical Atlantic (AL98):
Showers and thunderstorms associated with a low pressure area 
located about 350 miles east of Bermuda have changed little in 
organization during the past several hours.  Environmental 
conditions could support gradual development of the system during 
the next day or two, and a tropical depression could form during 
that time. The system is forecast to move slowly today, and then 
possibly turn southward on Friday over the subtropical Atlantic. By 
Sunday, environmental conditions are forecast to become less 
favorable for development.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...medium...40 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...medium...40 percent.
