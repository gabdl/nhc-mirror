Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
1000 AM PST Thu Nov 5 2026

For the eastern and central North Pacific east of 180 longitude:

1. Eastern Central Pacific (CP95): 
Showers and thunderstorms associated with an area of low pressure 
located several hundred miles to the southeast of the Big Island of 
Hawaii continue to become better organized. Environmental conditions 
are conducive for additional development, and a tropical depression 
is expected to develop, possibly as soon as later today. The system 
is forecast to move west-northwestward to westward at 10 to 15 mph, 
passing well south of the Hawaiian Islands. 
* Formation chance through 48 hours...high...90 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...90 percent.

2. Eastern East Pacific:
A broad area of low pressure offshore of Central America is 
producing a large area of showers and thunderstorms that remain 
disorganized. Gradual development of this system is still possible 
during the next several days, and a tropical depression could form 
by this weekend while the low drifts slowly offshore of the Central 
America coast. Regardless of development, this system could bring 
areas of heavy rain to portions of Central America through this 
weekend.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...medium...40 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...medium...50 percent.



Forecaster Evans/Papin

Para el Pacífico Norte oriental y central al este de 180 longitud

Pacífico Central Este (CP95): Lluvias y tormentas eléctricas
asociadas con un área de baja presión ubicada varios cientos de
millas al sureste de la Isla Grande de Hawái continúan organizándose
mejor. Las condiciones ambientales son propicias para un desarrollo
adicional, y se espera que se desarrolle una depresión tropical,
posiblemente tan pronto como hoy mismo. Se pronostica que el sistema
se desplazará hacia el oeste-noroeste a oeste a 10 a 15 mph, pasando
bien al sur de las Islas de Hawai.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 48 horas...alta...90 por ciento.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 7 días...alta...90 por ciento.

Este del Pacífico: Una amplia área de baja presión hacia el mar de
Centroamérica está produciendo una gran área de aguaceros y
tormentas eléctricas que permanecen desorganizadas. Todavía es
posible el desarrollo gradual de este sistema durante los próximos
días, y una depresión tropical podría formarse para este fin de
semana a medida que las deriva bajas hacia el mar de la costa de
Centroamérica. Independientemente del desarrollo del sistema, este
sistema podría generar áreas de lluvia intensa en partes de
Centroamérica hasta este fin de semana.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 48 horas...medio...40 por ciento.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 7 días...media...50 por ciento.

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
700 AM EST Thu Nov 5 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

1. Western and Central Atlantic:
A broad, non-tropical low pressure area located a few hundred miles 
south of Bermuda is producing some disorganized convection mainly to 
the east of its center. Environmental conditions could support this 
system gradually acquiring subtropical characteristics through this 
weekend while it moves slowly eastward over the western and central 
subtropical Atlantic. By early next week, environmental conditions 
are forecast to become unfavorable for further development. For more 
information on this system, including Gale Warnings, please see High 
Seas Forecasts issued by the National Weather Service. 
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...30 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...medium...50 percent.



High Seas Forecasts issued by the National Weather Service can be 
found under AWIPS header NFDHSFAT1, WMO header FZNT01 KWBC, and 
online at ocean.weather.gov/shtml/NFDHSFAT1.php


Forecaster Reinhart
