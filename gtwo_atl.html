Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS Central Pacific Hurricane Center Honolulu HI
Issued by NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
200 PM HST Fri Sep 18 2026

For the central North Pacific...between 140W and 180W:

1. Western East Pacific (EP98):
Showers and thunderstorms associated with an area of low pressure
located well southwest of the southern tip of the Baja California
Peninsula remain disorganized. Environmental conditions are
conducive for development, and a tropical depression is likely to
form this weekend as it moves westward at 10 to 15 mph across the
western East Pacific.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...high...70 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...80 percent.

2. Well East-Southeast of the Hawaiian Islands (Former TD 15-E):
Showers and thunderstorms associated with the remnants of Tropical
Depression Fifteen-E, located well east of the Hawaiian Islands,
remain disorganized. Environmental conditions appear conducive for
some development of this system next week as it moves westward to
west-southwestward, and a tropical depression could form well to the
south or southeast of the Hawaiian Islands by the middle to latter
part of next week.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...10 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...medium...40 percent.



Forecaster Pierce/Evans/Gibbs

Para el Pacífico Norte central…entre 140 y 180 longitud oeste

Oeste del Pacífico Este (EP98): Los aguaceros y tormentas eléctricas
asociados con un área de baja presión ubicada al suroeste del
extremo sur de la Península de Baja California seguirán siendo
desorganizados. Las condiciones ambientales son propicias para el
desarrollo y es probable que se forme una depresión tropical este
fin de semana, a medida que se mueve hacia el oeste a una velocidad
de 10 a 15 mph en el oeste del Pacífico Oriental.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 48 horas...alta...70 por ciento.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 7 días...alta...80 por ciento.

Bien este-sureste de las islas hawaianas (antiguo TD 15-E): Los
aguaceros y tormentas eléctricas asociadas con los remanentes de la
Depresión Tropical Quince-E, ubicados en el este de las islas
hawaianas, seguirán siendo desorganizados. Las condiciones
ambientales parecen propicias para que se desarrolle este sistema la
próxima semana, a medida que se mueve hacia el oeste y el suroeste,
y podría formarse una depresión tropical al sur o sureste de las
islas hawaianas a mediados o finales de la próxima semana.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 48 horas...baja...10 por ciento.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 7 días...medio...40 por ciento.

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
800 PM EDT Fri Sep 18 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

1. Eastern Subtropical Atlantic (AL99):
Showers and thunderstorms associated with the low pressure system 
located several hundred miles southwest of the Azores have become 
more concentrated during the past few hours, and a tropical 
depression could form later tonight or on Saturday. The system is 
expected to move slowly over the next couple of days remaining 
southwest of the Azores, followed by a turn to the southwest by 
early next week. Environmental conditions are forecast to become 
less favorable for development beginning Sunday night or on Monday. 
* Formation chance through 48 hours...high...80 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...80 percent.
