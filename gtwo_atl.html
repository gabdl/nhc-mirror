Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS Central Pacific Hurricane Center Honolulu HI
Issued by NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
200 AM HST Fri Nov 13 2026

For the central North Pacific...between 140W and 180W:

1. Well West-Southwest of the Hawaiian Islands (Remnants of Pena):
Showers and thunderstorms remain disorganized in association with
the remnants of Pena. In addition, the system still lacks a
well-defined circulation, and environmental conditions are only
marginally conducive for redevelopment. The system is expected to
continue moving westward, crossing the International Date Line later
today.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...10 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...10 percent.
