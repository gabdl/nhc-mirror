Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
200 PM EDT Sat Aug 29 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

1. East of the Leeward Islands (Remnants of Dolly):
Disorganized showers and thunderstorm continue in association with 
the remnants of Tropical Storm Dolly, located about 300 miles east 
of the Leeward Islands. However, there are no signs that the system 
is developing a closed circulation. Redevelopment of this system is 
unlikely while it moves westward to west-northwestward near 25 mph. 
The wave could bring locally heavy rains and gusty winds to 
portions of the Leeward Islands, the Virgin Islands and Puerto Rico 
over the next several days.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...near 0 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...10 percent.

2. Northeastern and North-Central Gulf of America:
Cloudiness and showers associated with a weak low pressure area 
centered about 125 miles south of Pensacola, Florida, have become a 
little better organized since yesterday.  Some additional slow 
development of this system is possible during the next few days as 
it drifts westward.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...near 0 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...20 percent.



Forecaster Beven
