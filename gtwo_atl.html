Issued by NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
200 AM HST Wed Nov 04 2026

For the central North Pacific...between 140W and 180W:

1. Well Southeast of the Hawaiian Islands (EP95):
An area of low pressure located over the western portion of the
East Pacific basin continues to produce disorganized shower and

few days while it moves westward to west-northwestward at 10 to 15
mph.

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
400 AM PST Wed Nov 4 2026

For the eastern and central North Pacific east of 180 longitude:

1. Well Southeast of the Hawaiian Islands (EP95): 
An area of low pressure located over the western portion of the 
East Pacific basin continues to produce disorganized shower and 
thunderstorm activity. Environmental conditions appear conducive 
for gradual development of this system, and a tropical depression 
is likely to form over the Central Pacific basin during the next 
few days while it moves westward to west-northwestward at 10 to 15 
mph.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...medium...50 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...70 percent.

2. Eastern East Pacific:
A broad area of low pressure is producing a large area of 
disorganized showers and thunderstorms offshore of the coast of 
Central America. Gradual development of this system is possible 
during the next several days, and a tropical depression could form 
by this weekend while the low drifts northward. Regardless of 
development, this system could bring areas of heavy rain to portions 
of Central America through this weekend.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...30 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...medium...50 percent.



Forecaster Papin/Pasch

Para el Pacífico Norte oriental y central al este de 180 longitud

Bien al sureste de las Islas de Hawái (EP95): Un área de baja
presión ubicada sobre la porción oeste de la cuenca del Pacífico
este continúa produciendo actividad de aguaceros y tormentas
eléctricas desorganizadas. Las condiciones ambientales parecen
propicias para el desarrollo gradual de este sistema, y es probable
que se forme una depresión tropical sobre la cuenca del Pacífico
Central durante los próximos días, mientras que se mueve hacia el
oeste a oeste-noroeste a 10 a 15 mph.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 48 horas...medio...50 por ciento.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 7 días...alta...70 por ciento.

Este del Pacífico Oriental: Una amplia área de baja presión está
produciendo una gran área de aguaceros desorganizados y tormentas
eléctricas frente a la costa de Centroamérica. Es posible que este
sistema se desarrolle gradualmente durante los próximos días, y una
depresión tropical podría formarse para este fin de semana a medida
que las aguas bajas se desplacen hacia el norte. Independientemente
del desarrollo del sistema, este sistema podría generar áreas de
lluvia intensa en partes de Centroamérica hasta este fin de semana.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 48 horas...baja...30 por ciento.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 7 días...media...50 por ciento.

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
700 AM EST Wed Nov 4 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

1. Western and Central Atlantic:
A broad area of low pressure is forming along a remnant frontal 
boundary a few hundred miles south of Bermuda. Environmental 
conditions could support this system gradually acquiring subtropical 
characteristics through this weekend while it moves slowly eastward 
over the western and central subtropical Atlantic. By early next 
week, environmental conditions are forecast to become unfavorable 
for further development. For more information on this system, 
including Gale Warnings, please see High Seas Forecasts issued by 
the National Weather Service. 
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...30 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...medium...50 percent.



High Seas Forecasts issued by the National Weather Service can be 
found under AWIPS header NFDHSFAT1, WMO header FZNT01 KWBC, and 
online at ocean.weather.gov/shtml/NFDHSFAT1.php


Forecaster Papin/Pasch
