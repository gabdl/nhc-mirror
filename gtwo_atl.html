1. Well Southeast of the Hawaiian Islands (EP95):
A broad area of low pressure located over the western portion of the

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
400 PM PST Tue Nov 3 2026

For the eastern and central North Pacific east of 180 longitude:

1. Well Southeast of the Hawaiian Islands (EP95): 
A broad area of low pressure located over the western portion of the 
East Pacific basin is producing disorganized showers and 
thunderstorms. Environmental conditions appear conducive for gradual 
development of this system, and a tropical depression is likely to 
form over the Central Pacific basin during the next few days while 
the system moves westward to west-northwestward at 10 to 15 mph.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...medium...50 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...70 percent.

2. Eastern East Pacific:
An elongated trough of low pressure is producing a large area of 
disorganized showers and thunderstorms offshore of the coast of 
Central America. Some slow development of this system is possible 
during the next few days while it drifts northward. Regardless of 
development, this system could bring areas of heavy rain to 
portions of Central America later this week and over the weekend.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...10 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...30 percent.



Forecaster Reinhart

Centro Nacional de Huracanes del SNM Miami FL
400 PM PST martes 3 de noviembre de 2026

Para el Pacífico Norte oriental y central al este de 180 longitud

Bien al sureste de las Islas de Hawái (EP95): Una amplia área de
baja presión ubicada sobre la porción oeste de la cuenca del
Pacífico este está produciendo aguaceros y tormentas desorganizadas.
Las condiciones ambientales parecen propicias para el desarrollo
gradual de este sistema, y es probable que se forme una depresión
tropical sobre la cuenca del Pacífico Central durante los próximos
días, mientras que el sistema se mueve hacia el oeste a
oeste-noroeste a 10 a 15 mph.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 48 horas...medio...50 por ciento.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 7 días...alta...70 por ciento.

Este del Pacífico Oriental: Una vaguada de baja presión está
produciendo una gran área de aguaceros y tormentas desorganizadas
hacia el mar frente a la costa de Centroamérica. Es posible que este
sistema se desarrolle lentamente durante los próximos días a medida
que se desplace hacia el norte. Independientemente del desarrollo
del sistema, este sistema podría generar áreas de lluvia intensa en
partes de Centroamérica a finales de esta semana y durante el fin de
semana.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 48 horas...baja...10 por ciento.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 7 días...baja...30 por ciento.

TTAA00 KNHC DDHHMM

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
700 PM EST Tue Nov 3 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

1. Western and Central Atlantic:
An area of low pressure is expected to form over the next day or so 
along a weakening front a few hundred miles south of Bermuda. This 
system could gradually acquire some subtropical characteristics late 
this week or this weekend while it moves slowly eastward over the 
western and central subtropical Atlantic. By early next week, 
environmental conditions are forecast to become unfavorable for 
further development. For more information on this system, including 
Gale Warnings, please see High Seas Forecasts issued by the National 
Weather Service. 
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...20 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...medium...40 percent.



High Seas Forecasts issued by the National Weather Service can be 
found under AWIPS header NFDHSFAT1, WMO header FZNT01 KWBC, and 
online at ocean.weather.gov/shtml/NFDHSFAT1.php


Forecaster Reinhart
