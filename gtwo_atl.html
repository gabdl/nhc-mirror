Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
500 PM PDT Tue Oct 20 2026

For the eastern and central North Pacific east of 180 longitude:

1. South of Southern Mexico (EP94):
A low-pressure system located a few hundred miles south of the Gulf 
of Tehuantepec is producing a large area of showers and 
thunderstorms that are gradually becoming better organized, and a 
tropical depression is forecast to form within the next day or so. 
This system is expected to move westward to west-northwestward at 
around 10 mph during the next few days, remaining well offshore of 
southern Mexico. For additional information, including Gale 
Warnings, see the High Seas Forecast issued by the National Weather 
Service.

* Formation chance through 7 days...high...90 percent.

ZCZC MIATWOAT ALL
TTAA00 KNHC DDHHMM

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
800 PM EDT Tue Oct 20 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

1. Offshore of the East Coast of the United States (AL93):
A storm-force non-tropical low pressure area located a few hundred 
miles off the mid-Atlantic United States coast is producing some 
shower and thunderstorm activity primarily west of its center.  
Satellite imagery indicates that the low is still frontal.  Over 
the next couple of days, the low could gradually lose its frontal 
features while it moves slowly north-northeastward over the 
Gulf Stream, allowing the system to briefly develop subtropical 
characteristics. By the end of the week, the system is expected to 
accelerate northeastward away from the the eastern United States, 
and environmental conditions are expected to become unfavorable 
for additional subtropical or tropical development.

Regardless of the status of the system, high winds, coastal 
flooding, and dangerous surf are expected along portions of the U.S. 
East Coast during the next few days. For more information, please 
consult statements and warnings from local National Weather Service 
offices and the Weather Prediction Center, along with the High Seas 
Forecasts and the Storm Warnings issued by the National Weather 
Service.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...medium...40 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...medium...40 percent.



High Seas Forecasts issued by the National Weather Service can be 
found under AWIPS header NFDHSFAT1, WMO header FZNT01 KWBC, and 
online at ocean.weather.gov/shtml/NFDHSFAT1.php


Forecaster Katz/Pasch

800 PM EDT martes 20 de octubre de 2026

Para el Atlántico Norte...Mar Caribe y el Golfo de América

Alta mar de la costa este de los Estados Unidos (AL93): Un área de
baja presión no tropical con fuerza de tormenta ubicada a unos
cientos de millas de la costa del Atlántico medio de los Estados
Unidos está produciendo alguna actividad de aguaceros y tormenta
principalmente al oeste de su centro. Las imágenes de satélite
indican que la baja todavía es frontal. Durante los próximos dos
días, la baja podría perder gradualmente sus características
frontales mientras que se mueve lentamente hacia el norte-noreste
sobre la corriente del golfo, permitiendo que el sistema desarrolle
brevemente características subtropicales. Para el final de la
semana, se espera que el sistema se acelere hacia el noreste lejos
del este de los Estados Unidos, y se espera que las condiciones
ambientales se vuelvan desfavorables para un desarrollo subtropical
o tropical adicional.

Independientemente del estado del sistema, se esperan fuertes
vientos, inundaciones costeras y oleaje peligroso a lo largo de
porciones de la costa este de los Estados Unidos durante los
próximos días. Para más información, consulte los comunicados y
avisos de las oficinas locales del Servicio Nacional de Meteorología
y el Centro de Predicción del Tiempo, junto con los Pronósticos de
Alta Mar y los Avisos de Tormenta emitidos por el Servicio Nacional
de Meteorología.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 48 horas...medio...40 por ciento.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 7 días...medio...40 por ciento.
