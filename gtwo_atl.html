Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
500 AM PDT Sat Sep 5 2026

For the eastern and central North Pacific east of 180 longitude:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Hurricane 
Lowell, located several hundred miles south-southwest of the 
Hawaiian Islands, on Hurricane Karina, located about 800 hundred 
miles east of the Hawaiian Islands, and on Hurricane Marie, located 
several hundred miles west of the southern tip of the Baja 
California Peninsula.

1. Offshore of Southwestern Mexico:
An area of low pressure is forecast to develop well offshore of 
southwestern Mexico early next week. Gradual development is possible 
thereafter, and a tropical depression could form during the second 
half of the week while the system moves generally west-northwestward 
at 10 to 15 mph.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...near 0 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...medium...60 percent.



Forecaster D. Zelinsky
