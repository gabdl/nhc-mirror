Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS Central Pacific Hurricane Center Honolulu HI
Issued by NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
200 AM HST Sun Sep 20 2026

For the central North Pacific...between 140W and 180W:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on newly formed
Tropical Storm Odalys, located located over a thousand miles
southwest of the southern tip of Baja California.

1. Well East-Southeast of the Hawaiian Islands (Former TD 15-E):
Showers and thunderstorms associated with the remnants of Tropical
Depression Fifteen-E, located well east-southeast of the Hawaiian
Islands, remain limited. Environmental conditions appear conducive
for some development of this system by the middle to latter portion
of this week as it moves westward to west-southwestward, and a
tropical depression could form to the south or southeast of the
Hawaiian Islands once the environment becomes more favorable.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...near 0 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...medium...60 percent.

header WTPZ31 KNHC and under AWIPS header MIATCPEP1.

header WTPZ21 KNHC and under AWIPS header MIATCMEP1.

Tropical Weather Outlook

For the eastern and central North Pacific east of 180 longitude:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on newly formed 
Tropical Storm Odalys, located located over a thousand miles 
southwest of the southern tip of Baja California.

1. Southwest of Southwestern Mexico (EP99):
Showers and thunderstorms associated with a broad area of low 
pressure located about 400 miles south of Manzanillo, Mexico have 
continued to become better organized over the past several hours. 
Environmental conditions remain conducive for development, and a 
tropical depression is likely to form within the next day or so as 
the system moves generally northward off the coast of southwestern 
Mexico. Interests along the southwestern and west-central coast of 
Mexico should monitor the progress of this system. For more 
information, including gale warnings, see the High Seas Forecast 
issued by the National Weather Service.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...high...90 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...90 percent.

Islands, remain limited. Environmental conditions appear conducive
