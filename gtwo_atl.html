Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS Central Pacific Hurricane Center Honolulu HI
Issued by NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
200 AM HST Mon Sep 28 2026

For the central North Pacific...between 140W and 180W:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Hurricane
Polo, located about 100 miles southwest of Cabo San Lazaro, Mexico,
on Hurricane Nolo, located a few hundred miles southwest of the main
Hawaiian Islands, and on Tropical Storm Rachel, located a few
hundred miles south-southwest of Acapulco, Mexico.

1. Well East-Southeast of the Hawaiian Islands (EP91):
A broad area of low pressure located well east-southeast of the
Hawaiian Islands continues to become better organized. Environmental
conditions are conducive for further development, and a tropical
depression is expected to form later today or tomorrow. The system
should move generally northeastward and remain well east of the
Hawaiian Islands.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...high...90 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...90 percent.

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
800 AM EDT Mon Sep 28 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Tropical 
Depression Fay, located well to the southwest of the Azores.

1. Central Subtropical Atlantic (AL91):
An area of low pressure located well to the east-northeast of 
Bermuda is moving quickly eastward. Recent microwave imagery 
suggests the shower and thunderstorm activity is beginning to show 
signs of organization, though it is unclear if the system has 
developed a well-defined circulation given its fast forward motion. 
While environmental conditions are currently only marginally 
favorable, this system is now likely to become a tropical depression 
or storm over the next day or two as it turns east-southeastward and 
slows down over the central Subtropical Atlantic. Environmental 
conditions are expected to become unfavorable for further 
development later this week.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...high...70 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...80 percent.



Forecaster Papin

Para el Atlántico Norte...Mar Caribe y el Golfo de América

Sistemas activos: El Centro Nacional de Huracanes está emitiendo
advertencias sobre la Depresión Tropical Fay, ubicada al suroeste de
las Azores.

Atlántico Subtropical Central (AL91): Un área de baja presión
ubicada al este-noreste de las Bermudas se está moviendo rápidamente
hacia el este. Imágenes recientes de microondas sugieren que la
actividad de aguaceros y tormentas está comenzando a mostrar signos
de organización, aunque no está claro si el sistema ha desarrollado
una circulación bien definida dado su rápido movimiento de avance.
Si bien las condiciones ambientales son actualmente solo
marginalmente favorables, es probable que este sistema se convierta
en una depresión tropical o tormenta en el próximo día o dos, a
medida que gire hacia el este-sureste y se ralentice sobre el
Atlántico Subtropical central. Se anticipa que las condiciones
ambientales se vuelvan desfavorables para un mayor desarrollo a
finales de esta semana.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 48 horas...alta...70 por ciento.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 7 días...alta...80 por ciento.
