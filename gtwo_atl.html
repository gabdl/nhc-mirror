Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS Central Pacific Hurricane Center Honolulu HI
Issued by NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
200 AM HST Thu Oct 29 2026

For the central North Pacific...between 140W and 180W:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Tropical
Storm Vance, located a couple hundred miles west-northwest of Cabo
Corrientes, Mexico.

1. Well Southeast of the Hawaiian Islands:
A broad area of low pressure is likely to develop well to the
southeast of the Hawaiian Islands this weekend or early next week.
Thereafter, environmental conditions appear generally favorable for
some slow development of this system as it moves westward to
west-northwestward into the Central Pacific basin.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...near 0 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...30 percent.



Forecaster Hagen

se mueve hacia el oeste a noroeste hacia la cuenca del Pacífico
Central.
