Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS Central Pacific Hurricane Center Honolulu HI
Issued by NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
800 AM HST Fri Sep 18 2026

For the central North Pacific...between 140W and 180W:

1. Western East Pacific (EP98):
Showers and thunderstorms associated with an area of low pressure
located almost 1000 miles south-southwest of the southern tip of
the Baja California Peninsula have become a little better organized
since yesterday, but the system's circulation remains broad and
elongated. Environmental conditions appear conducive for additional
development of this system, and a tropical depression is likely to
form this weekend as it moves westward at 10 to 15 mph across the
western East Pacific.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...high...70 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...80 percent.

2. Well East-Southeast of the Hawaiian Islands (Former TD 15-E):
Showers and thunderstorms associated with the remnants of Tropical
Depression Fifteen-E, located more than 1000 miles east-southeast
of the Hawaiian Islands, remain disorganized. Environmental
conditions appear conducive for some development of this system
next week as it moves westward to west-southwestward, and a
tropical depression could form well to the south or southeast of the
Hawaiian Islands by the middle to latter part of next week.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...10 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...medium...40 percent.



Forecaster Berg

Para el Pacífico Norte central…entre 140 y 180 longitud oeste

Pacífico Este Oeste (EP98): Los aguaceros y tormentas eléctricas
asociados con un área de baja presión ubicada casi 1000 millas al
sur-suroeste del extremo sur de la Península de Baja California
están un poco mejor organizados desde ayer, pero la circulación del
sistema sigue siendo amplia y alargada. Las condiciones ambientales
parecen propicias para un desarrollo adicional de este sistema, y es
probable que se forme una depresión tropical este fin de semana a
medida que se mueve hacia el oeste a una velocidad de 10 a 15 mph a
través del Pacífico oriental occidental.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 48 horas...alta...70 por ciento.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 7 días...alta...80 por ciento.

Bien este-sureste de las islas hawaianas (antiguo TD 15-E): Los
aguaceros y tormentas eléctricas asociadas con los remanentes de la
Depresión Tropical Quince-E, ubicados a más de 1000 millas al
este-sureste de las islas hawaianas, permanecen desorganizados. Las
condiciones ambientales parecen propicias para que se desarrolle
este sistema la próxima semana, a medida que se mueve hacia el oeste
y el suroeste, y podría formarse una depresión tropical al sur o
sureste de las islas hawaianas a mediados o finales de la próxima
semana.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 48 horas...baja...10 por ciento.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 7 días...medio...40 por ciento.

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
200 PM EDT Fri Sep 18 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

1. Eastern Subtropical Atlantic (AL99):
Showers and thunderstorms have changed little in organization since 
this morning in association with an area of low pressure located 
several hundred miles southwest of the Azores. However, just a small 
increase in organization is likely to result in the formation of a 
tropical depression as soon as later today. The system is expected 
to move slowly over the next couple of days remaining southwest of 
the Azores, followed by a turn to the southwest by early next week, 
when environmental conditions are forecast to become less favorable 
for additional development. 
* Formation chance through 48 hours...high...70 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...70 percent.



Forecaster Papin

Para el Atlántico Norte...Mar Caribe y el Golfo de América

Atlántico Subtropical Este (AL99): Los aguaceros y las tormentas
eléctricas han cambiado poco en su organización desde esta mañana, y

desplazarse al suroeste a principios de la próxima semana, cuando se
prevé que las condiciones ambientales se volverán menos favorables
para un mayor desarrollo.
