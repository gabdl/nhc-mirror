Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
1100 AM PDT Sat Oct 17 2026

For the eastern and central North Pacific east of 180 longitude:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Hurricane 
Trudy, located a couple of hundred miles west of Acapulco, Mexico.

1. South of Southern Mexico:
An area of low pressure is forecast to form offshore of southern 
Mexico during the early to middle part of next week. Environmental 
conditions appear conducive for gradual development thereafter, and 
a tropical depression is likely to form during the latter half of 
next week while it moves generally westward to west-northwestward 
over the eastern and central portions of the East Pacific. 
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...near 0 percent. 
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...70 percent.



Forecaster Adams
