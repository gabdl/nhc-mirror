ZCZC MIATWOEP ALL
TTAA00 KNHC DDHHMM

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
1100 PM PDT Mon Oct 12 2026

For the eastern and central North Pacific east of 180 longitude:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Hurricane 
Simon, located less than one hundred miles west of Cabo Corrientes, 
Mexico.

1. South of Southern Mexico (EP93):
A broad area of low pressure located several hundred miles 
south-southwest of the coast of Guatemala continues to show improved 
signs of organization with increased shower and thunderstorm 
activity. The environmental conditions are forecast to be conducive 
for further development of the system, and a tropical depression or 
tropical storm is expected to form well south of southern Mexico 
within the next day or so. The system is expected to move generally 
west-northwestward around 10 mph. Interests in southwestern Mexico 
should continue to monitor the progress of this system. For 
additional information, including Gale Warnings, see High Seas 
Forecasts issued by the National Weather Service. 
* Formation chance through 48 hours...high...near 100 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...near 100 percent.



High Seas Forecasts issued by the National Weather Service
can be found under AWIPS header NFDHSFEPI, WMO header FZPN02 
KWBC, and on the web at ocean.weather.gov/shtml/NFDHSFEPI.php


Forecaster Katz/Reinhart

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
200 AM EDT Tue Oct 13 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

1. Eastern Tropical Atlantic:
An elongated area of low pressure located several hundred miles west 
of the Cabo Verde Islands is producing a large area of disorganized 
showers and thunderstorms well to the east of its center. Further 
development of this system appears unlikely as the low moves slowly 
northward to north-northwestward and encounters stronger upper-level 
winds today and tonight. Regardless of development, heavy rainfall 
is expected to continue today across western portions of the Cabo 
Verde Islands with some flooding possible there.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...10 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...10 percent.



Forecaster Reinhart

Para el Atlántico Norte...Mar Caribe y el Golfo de América

Atlántico Tropical Este: Un área alargada de baja presión ubicada
