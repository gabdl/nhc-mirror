Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS Central Pacific Hurricane Center Honolulu HI
Issued by NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
200 AM HST Thu Oct 22 2026

For the central North Pacific...between 140W and 180W:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Tropical
Storm Vance, located several hundred miles south-southwest of
Acapulco, Mexico.

1. Western Central Pacific:
An area of low pressure is forecast to form near the International
Date Line, a few hundred miles west-southwest of Johnston Atoll
late this weekend or early next week.  Environmental conditions
appear conducive for some gradual development thereafter while the
system moves generally eastward to east-northeastward.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...near 0 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...30 percent.



Public advisories on Tropical Storm Vance are issued under WMO
header WTPZ32 KNHC and under AWIPS header MIATCPEP2.
Forecast/advisories on Tropical Storm Vance are issued under WMO
header WTPZ22 KNHC and under AWIPS header MIATCMEP2.


Forecaster Beven

ZCZC MIATWOAT ALL
TTAA00 KNHC DDHHMM

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
800 AM EDT Thu Oct 22 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Subtropical 
Storm Josephine, located a few hundred miles offshore of the 
Mid-Atlantic coast of the United States.

1. Subtropical Central Atlantic:
A trough of low pressure located several hundred miles to the 
east-southeast of Bermuda is producing a large area of disorganized 
showers and thunderstorms. Some development of this system is 
possible during the next day or two while it moves slowly 
north-northeastward. Upper-level winds are forecast to become too 
strong for further development by the weekend.  
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...10 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...10 percent.



Public Advisories on Josephine are issued under WMO header WTNT35 
KNHC and under AWIPS header MIATCPAT5.
Forecast/Advisories on Josephine are issued under WMO header WTNT25 
KNHC and under AWIPS header MIATCMAT5.


Forecaster Beven
