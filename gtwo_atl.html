For the eastern and central North Pacific east of 180 longitude:

1. Southwest of Southwestern Mexico:

system could become a tropical depression late this weekend or early 
next week while it moves slowly north-northwestward or northward.

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
800 PM EDT Wed Sep 16 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

1. Central Subtropical Atlantic (AL98):
A small area of low pressure located several hundred miles east of 
Bermuda continues to produce some disorganized showers and 
thunderstorms.  Although environmental conditions are marginal in 
the area, some additional development is possible over the next few 
days while the system moves slowly westward, and then possibly 
southward over the subtropical Atlantic.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...20 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...30 percent.
