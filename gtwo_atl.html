Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
400 AM PST Sat Nov 7 2026

For the eastern and central North Pacific east of 180 longitude:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Tropical 
Storm Pena, located several hundred miles south-southeast of the 
Big Island of Hawaii.

1. Eastern East Pacific:
A broad area of low pressure located offshore of Central America 
continues to produce a large area of showers and thunderstorms. Some 
development is possible over the next day or two, but unfavorable 
environmental conditions should end formation chances early next 
week. Regardless of development, this system is expected to bring 
areas of heavy rain to portions of Central America through the 
weekend while it drifts northward.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...30 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...30 percent.

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
100 AM EST Sat Nov 7 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

1. Western and Central Atlantic (AL94):
A non-tropical low pressure area located several hundred miles 
south-southeast of Bermuda is producing disorganized showers and 
thunderstorms. The low could still acquire some subtropical 
characteristics through tonight before upper-level winds increase 
on Sunday. The low is forecast to move generally eastward over the 
western and central subtropical Atlantic during the next couple of 
days and become absorbed by a new non-tropical low pressure system 
to its east early next week. Regardless, gale-force winds are 
likely over parts of the central subtropical Atlantic through the 
weekend. For more information, including Gale Warnings, see High 
Seas Forecasts issued by the National Weather Service.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...10 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...10 percent.



High Seas Forecasts issued by the National Weather Service can be 
found under AWIPS header NFDHSFAT1, WMO header FZNT01 KWBC, and 
online at ocean.weather.gov/shtml/NFDHSFAT1.php


Forecaster Beven

Para el Atlántico Norte...Mar Caribe y el Golfo de América

Atlántico Oeste y Central (AL94): Un área de baja presión no
tropical ubicada a varios cientos de millas al sureste de Bermuda
está produciendo aguaceros desorganizados y tormentas eléctricas. La
baja todavía podría adquirir algunas características subtropicales
hasta esta noche antes de que los vientos de nivel alto aumenten el
domingo. Se pronostica que la baja se desplazará generalmente hacia
el este sobre el Atlántico subtropical occidental y central durante
los próximos dos días y será absorbida por un nuevo sistema de baja
presión no tropical a su este a principios de la próxima semana. En
cualquier caso, es probable que vientos con fuerza de galerna sobre
partes del Atlántico subtropical central durante el fin de semana.
Para más información, incluyendo Avisos de Tormenta, vea Pronósticos
de Alta Mar emitidos por el Servicio Nacional de Meteorología.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 48 horas...baja...10 por ciento.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 7 días...baja...10 por ciento.
