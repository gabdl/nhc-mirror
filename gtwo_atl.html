Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS Central Pacific Hurricane Center Honolulu HI
Issued by NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
200 AM HST Sat Oct 24 2026

For the central North Pacific...between 140W and 180W:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Hurricane
Vance, located several hundred miles south-southwest of Acapulco,
Mexico.

1. Western Central Pacific:
A broad area of low pressure located near or just west of the
International Date Line, several hundred miles west of Johnston
Atoll, is producing disorganized showers and thunderstorms.
Environmental conditions could support some slow development while
the system moves generally eastward to east-northeastward.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...10 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...20 percent.



Forecaster Beven
