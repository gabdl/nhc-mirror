NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
500 PM PDT Fri Aug 28 2026

For the eastern and central North Pacific east of 180 longitude:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Tropical 
Storm Karina, located well south-southwest of the southern tip of 
the Baja California Peninsula, and on Tropical Storm Lowell, 
located well east-southeast of the Hawaiian Islands.

1. Well Offshore of Southwestern Mexico:
An area of disturbed weather, associated with a tropical wave, is 
located well to the south of southwestern Mexico. Environmental 
conditions appear favorable for gradual development of this system, 
and a tropical depression is likely to form early next week while 
it moves west-northwestward to northwestward at 10 to 15 mph.

Hawaiian Islands in a few days. Some gradual development is possible

part of next week while the system moves slowly west-northwestward, 
well offshore of the Hawaiian Islands.

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
800 PM EDT Fri Aug 28 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

1. East of the Leeward Islands (Remnants of Dolly):
A tropical wave, the remnants of Tropical Storm Dolly, are located 
several hundred miles east of the Leeward Islands.  Redevelopment 
of this system is unlikely while it moves westward to 
west-northwestward near 25 mph.  The wave could bring locally heavy 
rains and gusty winds to portions of the Leeward Islands, the 
Virgin Islands and Puerto Rico over the next several days.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...near 0 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...10 percent.

2. Northeastern and North-Central Gulf of America:
Disorganized cloudiness and showers over portions of the 
northeastern and north-central Gulf of America are associated with 
a weak area of low pressure.  Development, if any, of this system 
is expected to be slow while it remains nearly stationary for the 
next several days.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...near 0 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...10 percent.
