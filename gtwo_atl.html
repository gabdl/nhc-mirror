Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
1100 AM PDT Sun Aug 30 2026

For the eastern and central North Pacific east of 180 longitude:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Hurricane 
Karina, located well west-southwest of the southern tip of the Baja 
California Peninsula, and on Tropical Storm Lowell, located well 
east-southeast of the Hawaiian Islands.

1. Well Offshore of Southwestern Mexico:
A tropical wave with disorganized showers and thunderstorms, is 
located well to the south of southwestern Mexico. This system is 
expected to continue to move west-northwestward to northwestward at 
10 to 15 mph. Environmental conditions appear favorable for gradual 
development of this system, and a tropical depression is likely to 
form during the early to middle part of next week.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...high...70 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...90 percent.

2. Central Pacific:
An area of low pressure could form well south or southwest of the 
Hawaiian Islands in the next couple of days. Some gradual 
development is possible thereafter while it moves slowly westward 
across the central Pacific.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...near 0 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...30 percent.

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
800 AM EDT Sun Aug 30 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

1. East of the Leeward Islands (Remnants of Dolly):
A strong tropical wave, the remnants of Tropical Storm Dolly, is 
producing an area of showers and thunderstorms from Puerto Rico 
eastward across the Virgin Islands and the northern Leeward Islands. 
Satellite data and surface observations indicate that there are no 
signs of a closed surface circulation, and redevelopment is unlikely 
during the next day or two while the wave moves westward to 
west-northwestward at about 25 mph. The system should bring locally 
heavy rains and gusty winds to portions of the Leeward Islands, the 
Virgin Islands, and Puerto Rico over the next couple of days. 
Conditions may become more conducive for redevelopment by the middle 
of the week as the system reaches the southeastern Gulf of America.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...near 0 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...20 percent.

2. Northern Gulf of America (AL97):
Showers and thunderstorms associated with an area of low pressure 
located about 125 miles south of the coast of southeastern Louisiana 
have become more concentrated. However, current surface observations 
indicate that the system has not yet strengthened. Slow development 
of this system is possible during the next couple of days as it 
drifts westward to west-northwestward across the northern Gulf. 
Interests along the Louisiana and Upper Texas coasts should monitor 
the progress of this system.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...30 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...30 percent.



Forecaster Beven

Centro Nacional de Huracanes del SNM Miami FL

Para el Atlántico Norte...Mar Caribe y el Golfo de América

Al este de las Islas Leeward (Remanentes Dolly): Una fuerte ola
tropical, los remanentes de la Tormenta Tropical Dolly, está
produciendo un área de aguaceros y tormentas eléctricas desde Puerto
Rico hacia el este a través de las Islas Vírgenes y el norte de las
Islas Leeward. Los datos de satélite y las observaciones de
superficie indican que no hay signos de una circulación cerrada en
la superficie, y es poco probable que se produzca un nuevo
desarrollo durante el próximo día o dos, mientras que la ola se
mueve hacia el oeste a oeste-noroeste a alrededor de 25 mph. El
sistema debería traer lluvias intensas y vientos con ráfagas a
porciones de las Islas Sotavento, las Islas Vírgenes y Puerto Rico
durante los próximos dos días. Las condiciones pueden volverse más
propicias para el redesarrollo a mediados de la semana a medida que
el sistema alcance el sureste del Golfo de América.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 48 horas...baja...cerca del 0 por
ciento.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 7 días...baja...20 por ciento.

Norte del Golfo de América (AL97): Las lluvias y tormentas
eléctricas asociadas con un área de baja presión ubicada a unas 125
millas al sur de la costa del sureste de Louisiana se han vuelto más
concentradas. Sin embargo, las observaciones de superficie actuales
indican que el sistema aún no se ha fortalecido. Es posible que este
sistema se desarrolle lentamente durante los próximos dos días, a
medida que se desplaza hacia el oeste a través del norte del Golfo.
Los intereses a lo largo de las costas de Louisiana y Upper Texas
deben monitorear el progreso de este sistema.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 48 horas...baja...30 por ciento.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 7 días...baja...30 por ciento.

Pronosticador Beven
