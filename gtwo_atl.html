Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS Central Pacific Hurricane Center Honolulu HI
Issued by NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
200 AM HST Sat Sep 19 2026

For the central North Pacific...between 140W and 180W:

1. Western East Pacific (EP98):
Disorganized showers and thunderstorms continue to persist in
association with an area of low pressure located well southwest of
the southern tip of the Baja California Peninsula. Environmental
conditions are conducive for development, and a tropical depression
is expected to form over the next several days as it moves westward
to west-northwestward at 10 to 15 mph across the western East
Pacific.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...high...80 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...80 percent.

2. Well East-Southeast of the Hawaiian Islands (Former TD 15-E):
Showers and thunderstorms associated with the remnants of Tropical
Depression Fifteen-E, located well east of the Hawaiian Islands,
remain disorganized. Environmental conditions appear conducive for
some development of this system next week as it moves westward to
west-southwestward, and a tropical depression could form well to the
south or southeast of the Hawaiian Islands towards the middle to
latter portion of next week.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...10 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...medium...50 percent.



Forecaster Katz/Berg

Para el Pacífico Norte central…entre 140 y 180 longitud oeste

Pacífico Este Oeste (EP98): Continúan las precipitaciones y
tormentas eléctricas desorganizadas, así como un área de baja
presión situada al suroeste del extremo sur de la Península de Baja
California. Las condiciones ambientales son propicias para el
desarrollo y se espera que se forme una depresión tropical en los
próximos días a medida que se desplazará hacia el oeste a noroeste a
una velocidad de 10 a 15 mph a través del Pacífico oriental
occidental.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 48 horas...alta...80 por ciento.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 7 días...alta...80 por ciento.

Bien este-sureste de las islas hawaianas (antiguo TD 15-E): Los
aguaceros y tormentas eléctricas asociadas con los remanentes de la
Depresión Tropical Quince-E, ubicados en el este de las islas
hawaianas, seguirán siendo desorganizados. Las condiciones
ambientales parecen propicias para que se desarrolle este sistema la
próxima semana, a medida que se mueve hacia el oeste y el suroeste,
y podría formarse una depresión tropical al sur o sureste de las
islas de Hawái a mediados o finales de la próxima semana.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 48 horas...baja...10 por ciento.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 7 días...media...50 por ciento.

For the eastern and central North Pacific east of 180 longitude:

1. South-Southwest of Southwestern Mexico (EP99):
Recent satellite-derived wind data show that the area of low 
pressure located well south-southwest of the southwestern coast of 
Mexico is still elongated, and the shower and thunderstorm activity 
remains disorganized. However, environmental conditions are 
conducive for development, and a tropical depression is expected to 
form over the next couple of days as the system moves generally 
northward off the coast of southwestern Mexico. Interests along the 
southwestern and west-central coast of Mexico should monitor the 
progress of this system.

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
800 AM EDT Sat Sep 19 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

1. Eastern Subtropical Atlantic (AL99):
Showers and thunderstorms have become more concentrated near the 
center of a low pressure system located several hundred miles 
southwest of the western Azores since yesterday. A short-lived 
tropical depression or tropical storm is expected to form later 
today or tonight while moving north-northeastward at about 5 mph, 
but remaining well to the southwest of the Azores. Upper-level winds 
are forecast to become less conducive for development beginning 
late Sunday.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...high...90 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...90 percent.
