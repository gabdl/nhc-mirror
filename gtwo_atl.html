800 PM HST Wed Sep 23 2026

For the central North Pacific...between 140W and 180W:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Hurricane
Polo, located almost 100 miles offshore of southwestern Mexico, on
Hurricane Odalys, located well west-southwest of the southern tip of
Baja California, and on Tropical Storm Nolo, located a few hundred
miles south of the Big Island of Hawaii.

1. Well East-Southeast of the Hawaiian Islands:
An area of low pressure could form about 1000 miles east-southeast
of the Hawaiian Islands over the weekend. Environmental conditions
could support some gradual development of this system thereafter as
it moves generally northeastward, remaining well east-southeast of
the Hawaiian Islands.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...near 0 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...20 percent.

For the eastern and central North Pacific east of 180 longitude:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Hurricane 
Polo, located almost 100 miles offshore of southwestern Mexico, on 
Hurricane Odalys, located well west-southwest of the southern tip of 
Baja California, and on Tropical Storm Nolo, located a few hundred 
miles south of the Big Island of Hawaii.

1. South of the Gulf of Tehuantepec:

moves slowly west-northwestward to northwestward, parallel to the 
coast of southern and southwestern Mexico.

of the Hawaiian Islands over the weekend. Environmental conditions 
could support some gradual development of this system thereafter as 
it moves generally northeastward, remaining well east-southeast of 
the Hawaiian Islands.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...near 0 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...20 percent.

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
800 AM EDT Thu Sep 24 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Tropical 
Storm Fay, located several hundred miles southwest of the Azores.

1. Far Eastern Tropical Atlantic (AL90):
A broad area of low pressure located a few hundred miles 
south-southeast of the Cabo Verde Islands is producing a large 
area of disorganized showers and thunderstorms.  This system could 
become a short-lived tropical depression or a tropical storm later 
today or on Friday while it moves northwestward to northward.  
Increasing upper-level winds, however, should prevent further 
development by the weekend.  Interests in the Cabo Verde Islands 
should monitor the progress of this system as gusty winds, areas of 
heavy rain, and flash flooding are possible over the islands by 
Friday.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...high...70 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...70 percent.




Public Advisories on Tropical Storm Fay are issued under WMO 
header WTNT31 KNHC and under AWIPS header MIATCPAT1.  
Forecast/Advisories on Tropical Depression Fay are issued under WMO 
header WTNT21 KNHC and under AWIPS header MIATCMAT1.


Forecaster Roberts
