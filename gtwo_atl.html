Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
500 PM PDT Sun Oct 18 2026

For the eastern and central North Pacific east of 180 longitude:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Hurricane 
Trudy, located offshore of southwestern Mexico.

1. South of Southern Mexico:
An area of low pressure is developing well offshore to the south of 
the coasts of Guatemala and El Salvador. Environmental conditions 
appear conducive for gradual development of this system, and a 
tropical depression is expected to form around the middle of this 
week as the system moves westward to west-northwestward, remaining

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
200 PM EDT Sun Oct 18 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

1. Off the East Coast of the United States:
A storm-force non-tropical low pressure area is expected to form 
later today or tonight off the southeast coast of the United States. 
This low is likely to move slowly and erratically through the middle 
of this week, and some subtropical development is possible during 
that time. Regardless of development, this system will cause high 
winds, coastal flooding, and dangerous surf along portions of the 
U.S. East Coast. Please consult statements and warnings from local 
National Weather Service offices and the Weather Prediction Center 
for more information, along with High Seas Forecasts and Storm 
Warnings issued by the National Weather Service.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...near 0 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...30 percent.



High Seas Forecasts issued by the National Weather Service
can be found under AWIPS header NFDHSFAT1, WMO header FZNT01 
KWBC, and online at ocean.weather.gov/shtml/NFDHSFAT1.php


Forecaster Beven
