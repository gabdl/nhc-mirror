Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
500 AM PDT Mon Oct 19 2026

For the eastern and central North Pacific east of 180 longitude:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Tropical 
Storm Trudy, located a couple of hundred miles offshore of 
west-central Mexico.

1. South of Southern Mexico:
Showers and thunderstorms, associated with a trough of low pressure 
located several hundred miles south of Guatemala, remain 
disorganized. Environmental conditions appear conducive for 
development, and a tropical depression is expected to form around 
the middle of this week as the system moves westward to 
west-northwestward at around 10 mph, well to the south of the coast 
of southern Mexico.

* Formation chance through 7 days...high...90 percent.

ZCZC MIATWOAT ALL
TTAA00 KNHC DDHHMM

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
800 AM EDT Mon Oct 19 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

1. Off the East Coast of the United States:
A storm-force non-tropical low pressure area is located along a 
frontal boundary a few hundred miles off the southeast coast of the 
United States. This low is likely to move slowly and erratically 
over the next couple of days. If the low remains over the Gulf 
Stream and becomes detached from the front, it could briefly acquire 
some subtropical cyclone characteristics during the middle to latter 
portions of the week. The system is expected to begin to move 
northeastward away from the the eastern United States later this 
week, and environmental conditions are expected to become 
unfavorable for development by the weekend.  Regardless of the 
status of the system, high winds, coastal flooding, and dangerous 
surf are expected along portions of the U.S. East Coast during the 
next few days. Please consult statements and warnings from local 
National Weather Service offices and the Weather Prediction Center 
for more information, along with High Seas Forecasts and Storm 
Warnings issued by the National Weather Service.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...near 0 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...medium...40 percent.



High Seas Forecasts issued by the National Weather Service can be 
found under AWIPS header NFDHSFAT1, WMO header FZNT01 KWBC, and 
online at ocean.weather.gov/shtml/NFDHSFAT1.php


Forecaster Pasch

Perspectiva de tiempo tropical
Centro Nacional de Huracanes del SNM Miami FL
800 AM EDT lunes 19 de octubre de 2026

Para el Atlántico Norte...Mar Caribe y el Golfo de América

Frente a la costa este de los Estados Unidos: Se encuentra un área
de baja presión no tropical con fuerza de tormenta a lo largo de un
límite frontal a unos cientos de millas de la costa sureste de los
Estados Unidos. Es probable que esta baja se mueva lentamente y de
forma errática durante los próximos dos días. Si la baja permanece
sobre la corriente del golfo y se separa del frente, podría adquirir
brevemente algunas características de ciclón subtropical durante la
mitad o última hora de la semana. Se espera que el sistema comience
a desplazarse hacia el noreste lejos del este de los Estados Unidos
a finales de esta semana, y se espera que las condiciones
ambientales se vuelvan desfavorables para el desarrollo para el fin
de semana. Independientemente del estado del sistema, se esperan
fuertes vientos, inundaciones costeras y oleaje peligroso a lo largo
de porciones de la costa este de los Estados Unidos durante los
próximos días. Por favor, consulte los comunicados y avisos de las
oficinas locales del Servicio Nacional de Meteorología y el Centro
de Predicción del Tiempo para más información, junto con los
Pronósticos de Alta Mar y los Avisos de Tormenta emitidos por el
Servicio Nacional de Meteorología.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 48 horas...baja...cerca del 0 por
ciento.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 7 días...medio...40 por ciento.

&&
Los pronósticos de alta mar emitidos por el Servicio Nacional de
Meteorología se pueden encontrar bajo el encabezado de AWIPS
NFDHSFAT1, el encabezado de la OMM FZNT01 KWBC, y en línea en
ocean.weather.gov/shtml/NFDHSFAT1.php
