Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS Central Pacific Hurricane Center Honolulu HI
Issued by NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
800 PM HST Tue Sep 22 2026

For the central North Pacific...between 140W and 180W:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Hurricane
Polo, about 200 miles offshore of southern Mexico, and on Tropical
Storm Odalys, located well west-southwest of the southern tip of
Baja California.

1. Southeast of the Hawaiian Islands (Former TD 15-E):
Showers and thunderstorms associated with the remnants of Tropical
Depression Fifteen-E, located a few hundred miles south-southeast
the Big Island of Hawaii, have become better organized.
Environmental conditions remain conducive for development, and a
tropical depression could form tonight or Wednesday while the system
moves slowly west-northwestward. Heavy rains associated with this
system are forecast to spread over the southeastern Hawaiian Islands
starting on Wednesday and continuing through Saturday, possibly
resulting in life-threatening flash flooding and mudslides for
portions of the Big Island and Maui. Interests in the Hawaiian
Islands should continue to monitor the progress of this system.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...high...90 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...90 percent.



Forecaster Kelly

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
200 AM EDT Wed Sep 23 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

1. Far Eastern Tropical Atlantic:
A tropical wave is about to move off the west coast of Africa. An 
area of low pressure is expected to form in a day or so, and it 
could become a short-lived tropical depression on Thursday or 
Friday. Strong upper-level winds should end the opportunity for 
development by the weekend. Interests in the Cabo Verde Islands 
should monitor the progress of this system as gusty winds and areas 
of heavy rain are possible there by the end of the week.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...20 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...medium...40 percent.



Forecaster Cangialosi
