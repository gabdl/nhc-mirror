Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
1100 PM PDT Mon Oct 19 2026

For the eastern and central North Pacific east of 180 longitude:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center has issued the last advisory on  
Post-Tropical Cyclone Trudy, located a couple of hundred miles west 
of west-central Mexico.

1. South of Southern Mexico (EP94):
Satellite images indicate that the circulation of a low pressure 
system located a few hundred miles south of the Gulf of Tehuantepec 
is becoming better defined. The associated showers and 
thunderstorms are also beginning to show signs of organization, and 
a tropical depression is forecast to form during the next day or 
so. This system is expected to move westward to west-northwestward 
at around 10 mph during the next few days, remaining well offshore 
of southern Mexico.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...high...90 percent. 
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...90 percent.

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
200 AM EDT Tue Oct 20 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

1. Off the East Coast of the United States (AL93):
A storm-force non-tropical low pressure area remains attached 
to an occluded frontal boundary a few hundred miles off the North 
Carolina coast. This low could develop some organized deep 
convection and gradually lose its frontal features over the next 
couple of days while it moves slowly north-northeastward over the 
Gulf Stream. By the end of the week, the system is expected to
accelerate northeastward away from the the eastern United States, 
where environmental conditions are expected to become unfavorable 
for subtropical or tropical development.

Regardless of the status of the system, high winds, coastal 
flooding, and dangerous surf are expected along portions of the U.S. 
East Coast during the next few days. For more information, please 
consult statements and warnings from local National Weather Service 
offices and the Weather Prediction Center, along with High Seas 
Forecasts and Storm Warnings issued by the National Weather Service.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...30 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...medium...40 percent.



High Seas Forecasts issued by the National Weather Service can be 
found under AWIPS header NFDHSFAT1, WMO header FZNT01 KWBC, and 
online at ocean.weather.gov/shtml/NFDHSFAT1.php


Forecaster Cangialosi

Perspectiva de tiempo tropical
Centro Nacional de Huracanes del SNM Miami FL
200 AM EDT martes 20 de octubre de 2026

Para el Atlántico Norte...Mar Caribe y el Golfo de América

Frente a la costa este de los Estados Unidos (AL93): Un área de baja
presión no tropical con fuerza de tormenta permanece unida a un
límite frontal ocluido a unos cientos de millas de la costa de
Carolina del Norte.Esta baja podría desarrollar alguna convección
profunda organizada y perder gradualmente sus características
frontales en los próximos dos días mientras se mueve lentamente
hacia el norte-noreste sobre la corriente del golfo. Para el final
de la semana, se espera que el sistema se acelere hacia el noreste
lejos del este de los Estados Unidos, donde se espera que las
condiciones ambientales se vuelvan desfavorables para el desarrollo
subtropical o tropical.

Independientemente del estado del sistema, se esperan fuertes
vientos, inundaciones costeras y oleaje peligroso a lo largo de
porciones de la costa este de los Estados Unidos durante los
próximos días. Para más información, consulte los comunicados y
avisos de las oficinas locales del Servicio Nacional de Meteorología
y el Centro de Predicción del Tiempo, así como los Pronósticos de
Alta Mar y los Avisos de Tormenta emitidos por el Servicio Nacional
de Meteorología.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 48 horas...baja...30 por ciento.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 7 días...medio...40 por ciento.

&&
Los pronósticos de alta mar emitidos por el Servicio Nacional de
Meteorología se pueden encontrar bajo el encabezado de AWIPS
NFDHSFAT1, el encabezado de la OMM FZNT01 KWBC, y en línea en
ocean.weather.gov/shtml/NFDHSFAT1.php
