Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
500 AM PDT Tue Oct 6 2026

For the eastern and central North Pacific east of 180 longitude:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Hurricane 
Rachel, located several hundred miles west-southwest of the 
southern tip of the Baja California Peninsula.

1. South of Southern Mexico (EP92):
Shower and thunderstorm coverage has been increasing in association 
with a broad area of low pressure located offshore of southern 
Mexico. Environmental conditions appear favorable for gradual 
development, and a tropical depression is likely to form within the 
next day or two while the system moves slowly west-northwestward to 
northwestward. Regardless of development, heavy rainfall is expected 
across portions of southern and southwestern Mexico over the next 
few days. Interests along the coast of southwestern Mexico should 
monitor the progress of this system.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...high...90 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...90 percent.

2. South of Guatemala:
An area of low pressure is expected to form south of the coast of 
Guatemala early next week. Environmental conditions appear conducive 
for some gradual development of this system thereafter while it 
moves generally west-northwestward around 10 mph.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...near 0 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...20 percent.



Forecaster Kelly

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
800 AM EDT Tue Oct 6 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

1. Southwestern Gulf of America (AL92):
Showers and thunderstorms are showing signs of organization in 
association with a broad area of low pressure located over the 
southwestern Gulf of America. Continued development of this system 
is expected, and a tropical depression is anticipated to form over 
the next day or so while it drifts eastward. The low pressure area 
could bring gusty winds and areas of heavy rain to the western 
portions of the Yucatan Peninsula of Mexico in a couple of days.

The disturbance is expected to turn to the north or northeast toward 
the northern Gulf Coast by the end of the week. It is still too 
early to determine specific impacts to the northern Gulf Coast, but 
interests from the Florida Panhandle to Louisiana should closely 
monitor the progress of this system. The NOAA Gulfstream is 
scheduled to sample the environment over the Gulf later today and an 
Air Force reconnaissance aircraft is scheduled to investigate the 
system tomorrow.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...high...90 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...90 percent.



Forecaster Cangialosi

Para el Atlántico Norte...Mar Caribe y el Golfo de América

Suroeste del Golfo de América (AL92): Lluvias y tormentas eléctricas
están mostrando signos de organización en asociación con una amplia
área de baja presión ubicada sobre el suroeste del Golfo de América.
Se espera el desarrollo continuo de este sistema, y se anticipa que
se forme una depresión tropical durante el próximo día más o menos
mientras se desplaza hacia el este. El área de baja presión podría
traer ráfagas de viento y áreas de fuertes lluvias a las porciones
oeste de la Península de Yucatán de México en un par de días.

Se espera que la perturbación gire al norte o noreste hacia la costa
norte del Golfo para finales de la semana. Todavía es demasiado
pronto para determinar impactos específicos en la costa norte del
Golfo, pero los intereses desde el Panhandle de Florida a Louisiana
deben monitorear de cerca el progreso de este sistema. La corriente
del Golfo de la NOAA está programada para tomar muestras del medio
ambiente sobre el Golfo más tarde hoy y un avión de reconocimiento
de la Fuerza Aérea está programado para investigar el sistema
mañana.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 48 horas...alta...90 por ciento.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 7 días...alta...90 por ciento.
