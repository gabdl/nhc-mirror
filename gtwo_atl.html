Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
500 AM PDT Wed Sep 23 2026

For the eastern and central North Pacific east of 180 longitude:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Hurricane 
Polo, located a couple of hundred miles offshore of southern 
Mexico, on recently upgraded Hurricane Odalys, located well 
west-southwest of the southern tip of Baja California, and on 
recently re-formed Tropical Depression Fifteen-E, located a few 
hundred miles south-southeast of the Big Island of Hawaii.

1. South of the Gulf of Tehuantepec:
An area of low pressure is expected to form in a couple of days to 
the south of the Gulf of Tehuantepec. Afterwards, environmental 
conditions should be favorable for gradual development, and a 
tropical depression is likely to form late this week or this weekend 
as the system moves slowly west-northwestward to northwestward, 
parallel to the coast of southern and southwestern Mexico.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...near 0 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...80 percent.

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
800 AM EDT Wed Sep 23 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

1. Far Eastern Tropical Atlantic:
An area of low pressure, associated with a tropical wave that just 
moved off the west coast of Africa, has formed about 600 miles 
southeast of the Cabo Verde Islands.  This system could become a 
short-lived tropical depression on Thursday or Friday while it 
moves northwestward to northward.  However, strong upper-level 
winds should end the opportunity for development by the weekend.  
Interests in the Cabo Verde Islands should monitor the progress of 
this system as gusty winds and areas of heavy rain are possible 
there by the end of the week.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...30 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...medium...40 percent.

2. Central Subtropical Atlantic (Former Tropical Storm Fay):
A low pressure system, formerly Tropical Storm Fay, is located 
about 800 miles southwest of the Azores.  Shower activity 
associated with the low has increased this morning.  However, 
significant re-development of this system is unlikely while it 
moves generally westward over the next couple of days.  By the
weekend, increasingly strong upper-level winds should inhibit 
re-development.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...20 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...20 percent.

Para el Atlántico Norte...Mar Caribe y el Golfo de América

Lejano Oriente, el Atlántico Tropical: Un área de baja presión,
asociada con una onda tropical que se acaba de mover de la costa
oeste de África, se ha formado a unas 600 millas al sureste de las
Islas de Cabo Verde. Este sistema podría convertirse en una
depresión tropical de corta duración el jueves o el viernes mientras
se mueve del noroeste al norte. Sin embargo, fuertes vientos de
nivel superior deberían poner fin a la oportunidad de desarrollo
para el fin de semana. Las personas con interés en las Islas de Cabo
Verde deben monitorear el progreso de este sistema, ya que son
posibles ráfagas de viento y áreas de lluvia intensa allí para el
final de la semana.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 48 horas...baja...30 por ciento.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 7 días...medio...40 por ciento.

Atlántico Subtropical Central (Antigua Tormenta Tropical Fay): Un
sistema de baja presión, anteriormente Tormenta Tropical Fay, se
encuentra a unas 800 millas al suroeste de las Azores. La actividad
de aguaceros asociada con la baja ha aumentado esta mañana. Sin
embargo, es poco probable que se produzca un nuevo desarrollo
significativo de este sistema, ya que se mueve generalmente hacia el
oeste durante los próximos dos días. Para el fin de semana, vientos
de nivel alto cada vez más fuertes deberían inhibir el redesarrollo.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 48 horas...baja...20 por ciento.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 7 días...baja...20 por ciento.
