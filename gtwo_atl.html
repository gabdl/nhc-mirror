Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
1100 AM PDT Sun Oct 11 2026

For the eastern and central North Pacific east of 180 longitude:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Hurricane 
Simon, located just offshore of southwestern Mexico, and has issued 
the final advisory on the remnants of Rachel, located inland over 
Nevada.

1. South of Southern Mexico:
A tropical wave over the far eastern East Pacific is producing 
disorganized showers and thunderstorms. Environmental conditions are 
expected to be conducive for gradual development, and a tropical 
depression is expected to form well south of southern Mexico by the 
middle or latter half of the week as the system moves generally 
west-northwestward at around 10 mph.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...30 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...90 percent.

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
200 PM EDT Sun Oct 11 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

1. Eastern Tropical Atlantic:
An elongated area of low pressure centered a few hundred miles 
southwest of the Cabo Verde Islands is producing a large but 
disorganized area of showers and thunderstorms, extending from these 
islands southwestward into the eastern Tropical Atlantic. 
Environmental conditions appear only marginally conducive for some 
development of this system over the next day or two before 
upper-level winds become too strong for any further development by 
midweek. Regardless, heavy rainfall is expected over the next few 
days for portions of the Cabo Verde Islands as the system moves 
slowly northward to north-northwestward.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...20 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...20 percent.



Forecaster Evans/Papin

Para el Atlántico Norte...Mar Caribe y el Golfo de América

Atlántico Tropical Este: Un área alargada de baja presión centrada a
unos cientos de millas al suroeste de las Islas Cabo Verde está
produciendo un área grande pero desorganizada de aguaceros y
tormentas eléctricas, que se extiende desde estas islas hacia el
suroeste hasta el Atlántico Tropical este. Las condiciones
ambientales parecen solo marginalmente propicias para algún

que los vientos de nivel superior se vuelvan demasiado fuertes para
cualquier desarrollo adicional a mediados de semana. En cualquier
caso, se espera que las fuertes lluvias se produzcan en los próximos
días en partes de las Islas Cabo Verde, a medida que el sistema se
mueve lentamente hacia el norte a nornoroeste.
