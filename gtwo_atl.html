Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS Central Pacific Hurricane Center Honolulu HI
Issued by NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
800 AM HST Thu Sep 17 2026

For the central North Pacific...between 140W and 180W:

1. Western East Pacific:
A tropical wave located well to the south-southwest of the
southern tip of the Baja California Peninsula is showing signs of
organization. Environmental conditions appear conducive for
additional development of this system, and a tropical depression is
likely to form this weekend or early next week as it moves westward
at at 10 to 15 mph across the western part of the East Pacific.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...medium...40 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...80 percent.

2. Well East-Southeast of the Hawaiian Islands (Former TD15-E):
Showers and thunderstorms continue in association with the remnants
of Tropical Depression Fifteen-E. Environmental conditions appear
only marginally conducive for some development of this system as it
moves gradually westward, remaining east-southeast of the Hawaiian
Islands.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...10 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...20 percent.



Forecaster Wegman/Mahoney/Blake/Snell

Perspectiva de tiempo tropical
Centro de Huracanes del Pacífico Central del SNM Honolulu HI
Emitido por el Centro Nacional de Huracanes del SNM Miami FL
800 AM HST jueves, 17 de septiembre de 2026

Para el Pacífico Norte central…entre 140 y 180 longitud oeste

Oeste del Pacífico Oriental: Una onda tropical ubicada hasta el
sur-suroeste del extremo sur de la Península de Baja California
muestra signos de organización. Las condiciones ambientales parecen
propicias para el desarrollo adicional de este sistema, y es
probable que se forme una depresión tropical este fin de semana o a
principios de la próxima semana, a medida que se mueve hacia el
oeste a una velocidad de 10 a 15 mph a través de la parte oeste del
Pacífico este.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 48 horas...media...40 por ciento.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 7 días...alta...80 por ciento.

Bien este-sureste de las islas de Hawái (antiguo TD15-E): Continúan
las lluvias y tormentas eléctricas y los remanentes de la Depresión
Tropical Quince-E. Las condiciones ambientales parecen propiciar
solo marginalmente el desarrollo de este sistema, ya que se
desplazará gradualmente hacia el oeste, quedando en el este-sureste
de las islas hawaianas.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 48 horas...baja...10 por ciento.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 7 días...baja...20 por ciento.

$$
Pronosticador Wegman/Mahoney/Blake/Snell

ZCZC MIATWOEP ALL
TTAA00 KNHC DDHHMM

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
500 PM PDT Thu Sep 17 2026

For the eastern and central North Pacific east of 180 longitude:

1. Western East Pacific (EP98):
Showers and thunderstorms associated with a broad area of low 
pressure located well west-southwest of the southern tip of the Baja 
California Peninsula remain disorganized. Environmental conditions 
appear conducive for additional development of this system, and a 
tropical depression is likely to form this weekend as it moves 
westward at 10 to 15 mph across the western part of the East Pacific 
and into the central Pacific basin.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...medium...40 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...80 percent.

2. Well East-Southeast of the Hawaiian Islands (Former TD15-E):
Showers and thunderstorms continue in association with the remnants 
of Tropical Depression Fifteen-E. Environmental conditions appear 
only marginally conducive for some development of this system as it 
moves gradually westward, remaining east-southeast of the Hawaiian 
Islands. 
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...10 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...20 percent.

3. South of Southwestern Mexico:
Disorganized showers and thunderstorms continue with a tropical wave 
located several hundred miles south of Southwestern Mexico. 
Environmental conditions remain conducive for gradual development of 
this system, and a tropical depression is likely to form late this 
weekend or early next week while it moves gradually northwestward 
and then northward off the coast of southwestern Mexico.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...near 0 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...80 percent.



Forecaster Pierce/Evans/Gibbs

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

1. Central Subtropical Atlantic (AL98):
