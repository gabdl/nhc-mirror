Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS Central Pacific Hurricane Center Honolulu HI
Issued by NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
200 AM HST Fri Oct 23 2026

For the central North Pacific...between 140W and 180W:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on recently
upgraded Hurricane Vance, located several hundred miles
south-southwest of Acapulco, Mexico.

1. Western Central Pacific:
An area of low pressure is forecast to form near the International
Date Line, several hundred miles west of Johnston Atoll late this
weekend or early next week. Environmental conditions could support
some slow development thereafter while the system moves generally
eastward to east-northeastward.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...near 0 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...20 percent.



Forecaster Beven
