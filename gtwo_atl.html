Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS Central Pacific Hurricane Center Honolulu HI
Issued by NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
200 PM HST Sun Sep 27 2026

For the central North Pacific...between 140W and 180W:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Hurricane
Polo, located southwest of Cabo San Lucas, Mexico, on Hurricane
Nolo, located a few hundred miles south-southwest of the Big Island
of Hawaii, on recently upgraded Tropical Storm Rachel, located
several hundred miles southwest of the Acapulco, Mexico, and has
issued the last advisory on Post-Tropical Cyclone Odalys, located
well west of the southern tip of Baja California.

1. Well East-Southeast of the Hawaiian Islands:
A broad area of low pressure located well east-southeast of the
Hawaiian Islands is producing a large area of showers and
thunderstorms. Recent satellite-derived wind data indicate that the
system is becoming better organized. Environmental conditions are
expected to be conducive for further development, and a tropical
depression is likely to form during the next day or two. The system
should move generally northeastward and remain well east-southeast
of the Hawaiian Islands.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...high...70 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...90 percent.



Public Advisories on Tropical Storm Rachel are issued under
WMO header WTPZ33 KNHC and under AWIPS header MIATCPEP3.
Forecast/Advisories on Tropical Storm Rachel are issued
under WMO header WTPZ23 KNHC and under AWIPS header MIATCMEP3.


Forecaster Kelly

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
800 PM EDT Sun Sep 27 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Tropical 
Depression Fay, located well to the west-southwest of the Azores.

1. Central Subtropical Atlantic (AL91):
A trough of low pressure located several hundred miles 
east-northeast of Bermuda is moving quickly northeastward. This 
system is producing disorganized showers and thunderstorms, and it 
could become a tropical depression in the next couple of days when 
it turns eastward and slows down over the central Atlantic.  
Environmental conditions are expected to become unfavorable for 
development late this week.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...medium...50 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...medium...60 percent.
