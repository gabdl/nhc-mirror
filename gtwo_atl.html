Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
1100 PM PDT Mon Sep 14 2026

For the eastern and central North Pacific east of 180 longitude:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Tropical 
Depression Norbert, located well east of the Hawaiian Islands, and 
on Tropical Depression Fifteen-E, located well west-southwest of the 
southern tip of the Baja California Peninsula.

1. Southwest of Southwestern Mexico:
An area of low pressure could form well to the south of southwestern 
Mexico by the end of the week or early next week. Environmental 
conditions appear conducive for some gradual development thereafter 
while the system moves west-northwestward to northwestward at about 
10 mph.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...near 0 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...20 percent.



Forecaster Kelly

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
200 AM EDT Tue Sep 15 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

1. Central Subtropical Atlantic:
A trough of low pressure located well east-southeast of Bermuda 
continues to produce disorganized showers and thunderstorms. 
Environmental conditions are forecast to become more conducive for 
development in a few days, and a tropical depression could form late 
this week as the system moves slowly west-northwestward over the 
subtropical Atlantic.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...near 0 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...medium...40 percent.



Forecaster Kelly
