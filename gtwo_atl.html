Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS Central Pacific Hurricane Center Honolulu HI
Issued by NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
800 AM HST Mon Sep 28 2026

For the central North Pacific...between 140W and 180W:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Hurricane
Polo, located less than one hundred miles west-southwest of Cabo San
Lazaro, Mexico, on Hurricane Nolo, located a few hundred miles
southwest of the main Hawaiian Islands, and on Tropical Storm
Rachel, located a few hundred miles south-southwest of Acapulco,
Mexico.

1. Well East-Southeast of the Hawaiian Islands (EP91):
Showers and thunderstorms associated with an area of low pressure
located well east-southeast of the Hawaiian Islands have changed
little in organization this morning. Environmental conditions are
conducive for further development, and a tropical depression is
expected to form later today or tomorrow. The system should move
generally northeastward and remain well east of the Hawaiian
Islands.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...high...90 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...90 percent.



Forecaster Adams

800 AM HST lunes 28 de septiembre de 2026

Para el Pacífico Norte central…entre 140 y 180 longitud oeste

Sistemas activos: El Centro Nacional de Huracanes está emitiendo
advertencias sobre el Huracán Polo, ubicado a menos de cien millas
al oeste-suroeste de Cabo San Lazaro, México, sobre el Huracán Nolo,
ubicado a unos cientos de millas al suroeste de las principales
Islas de Hawai, y sobre la Tormenta Tropical Rachel, ubicada a unos
cientos de millas al sur-suroeste de Acapulco, México.

Bien este-sureste de las Islas Hawaii (EP91): Los aguaceros y las
tormentas eléctricas asociadas con un área de baja presión ubicada
bien al este-sureste de las Islas Hawaii han cambiado poco en
organización esta mañana. Las condiciones ambientales son propicias
para un mayor desarrollo, y se espera que se forme una depresión
tropical más tarde hoy o mañana. El sistema debe moverse
generalmente hacia el noreste y permanecer a un buen nivel al este
de las Islas Hawaii.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 48 horas...alta...90 por ciento.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 7 días...alta...90 por ciento.

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
200 PM EDT Mon Sep 28 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Tropical 
Depression Fay, located well to the west-southwest of the Azores and 
on newly formed Tropical Storm Hanna, located well to the 
east-northeast of Bermuda. 

Tropical cyclone formation is not expected over the next 7 days.

&&
Public Advisories on Tropical Storm Hanna are issued under WMO 
header WTNT33 KNHC and under AWIPS header MIATCPAT3. 
Forecast/Advisories on Tropical Storm Hanna are issued under WMO 
header WTNT23 KNHC and under AWIPS header MIATCMAT3.

$$
