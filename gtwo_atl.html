1. Well Southeast of the Hawaiian Islands:
A broad area of low pressure located well east-southeast of the

likely to form during the next few days as it moves westward to
west-northwestward around 10 to 15 mph into the Central Pacific
basin.

ZCZC MIATWOAT ALL
TTAA00 KNHC DDHHMM

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
700 AM EST Tue Nov 3 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

1. Western Atlantic:
An area of low pressure is expected to form along a weakening 
frontal boundary a few hundred miles south of Bermuda over the next 
day or so. This system could acquire some subtropical 
characteristics late this week while it drifts slowly over the 
western Atlantic. The chances of subtropical development decrease by 
this weekend when the system is expected to move generally eastward 
into unfavorable environmental conditions.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...10 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...30 percent.



Forecaster Papin
