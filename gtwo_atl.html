Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
1100 AM PDT Fri Oct 16 2026

For the eastern and central North Pacific east of 180 longitude:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Tropical 
Storm Simon, located a few hundred miles south of the southern tip 
of the Baja California Peninsula, and on Hurricane Trudy, located a 
few hundred miles southwest of Acapulco, Mexico.

1. South of Southern Mexico:
An area of low pressure is forecast to form offshore of southern 
Mexico during the early to middle part of next week. Environmental 
conditions appear conducive for gradual development thereafter, and 
a tropical depression could form during the latter half of next week 
while it moves generally westward to west-northwestward over the 
eastern and central portions of the East Pacific. 
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...near 0 percent. 
* Formation chance through 7 days...medium...60 percent.



Forecaster Adams
