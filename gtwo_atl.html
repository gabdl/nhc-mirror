Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
200 PM EDT Mon Aug 31 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on newly formed 
Tropical Depression Five, located over the northern Gulf of America.

1. Near Hispaniola (Remnants of Dolly):
A surface trough of low pressure (the remnants of Tropical Storm 
Dolly) is producing disorganized showers and thunderstorms near and 
over portions of Hispaniola, Turks and Caicos, and the southeastern 
Bahamas. Significant development of this system is no longer 
expected while it moves west-northwestward at 15 to 20 mph over the 
southwestern Atlantic. Regardless, the system could bring locally 
heavy rains and gusty winds to portions of these areas during the 
next couple of days.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...near 0 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...near 0 percent.



Public Advisories on Tropical Depression Five are issued under WMO 
header WTNT35 KNHC and under AWIPS header MIATCPAT5.
Forecast/Advisories on Tropical Depression Five are issued under WMO 
header WTNT25 KNHC and under AWIPS header MIATCMAT5.
