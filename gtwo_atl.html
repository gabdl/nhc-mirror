Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
1100 PM PDT Fri Oct 9 2026

For the eastern and central North Pacific east of 180 longitude:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Tropical 
Storm Rachel, located several hundred miles west-northwest of the 
southern tip of the Baja California Peninsula, and on Hurricane 
Simon, located about one hundred miles south of Manzanillo, Mexico.

1. South of Guatemala:
An area of low pressure is forecast to form south of the coast of 
Guatemala early next week. Environmental conditions are expected to 
be conducive for gradual development thereafter, and a tropical 
depression is likely to form by the middle or latter half of next 
week as the system moves generally west-northwestward at around 10 
mph.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...near 0 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...70 percent.



Forecaster Evans/Gibbs
