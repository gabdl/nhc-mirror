Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
500 PM PDT Mon Oct 12 2026

For the eastern and central North Pacific east of 180 longitude:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Hurricane 
Simon, located within a hundred miles west of Cabo Corrientes, 
Mexico.

1. South of Southern Mexico (EP93):
Showers and thunderstorms are beginning to show signs of 
organization in association with a broad area of low pressure 
located several hundred miles south-southwest of the coast of 
Guatemala. This system is gradually becoming better defined, and 
environmental conditions are forecast to be conducive for further 
development. A tropical depression or tropical storm is expected to 
form well south of southern Mexico later tonight or tomorrow while 
the system moves generally west-northwestward at around 10 mph.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...high...near 100 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...near 100 percent.



Forecaster Pierce/Cangialosi

ZCZC MIATWOAT ALL

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
800 PM EDT Mon Oct 12 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

1. Eastern Tropical Atlantic:
An elongated area of low pressure centered several hundred miles 
west of the Cabo Verde Islands continues to produce a large, but 
disorganized area of showers and thunderstorms on its east side, 
extending toward the westernmost Cabo Verde Islands. Environmental 
conditions appear, at best, only marginally conducive for 
development of this system before upper-level winds become too 
strong for any further development on Tuesday. Regardless, heavy 
rainfall is expected to continue for the next day or two across 
western portions of the Cabo Verde Islands, and some flooding is 
possible there as the system moves slowly northward to 
north-northwestward.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...20 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...20 percent.



Forecaster Adams

Para el Atlántico Norte...Mar Caribe y el Golfo de América

Atlántico Tropical Este: Un área alargada de baja presión centrada a
varios cientos de millas al oeste de las Islas Cabo Verde continúa
produciendo un área grande, pero desorganizada de aguaceros y
tormentas en su lado este, extendiéndose hacia las islas de Cabo
Verde más al oeste. Las condiciones ambientales parecen estar, en el
mejor de los casos, solo marginalmente propicias para el desarrollo
de este sistema antes de que los vientos de nivel superior se
vuelvan demasiado fuertes para cualquier otro desarrollo el martes.
En cualquier caso, se espera que las fuertes lluvias continúen
durante el próximo día o dos a través de las porciones occidentales
de las Islas Cabo Verde, y es posible que se produzcan inundaciones
a medida que el sistema se mueve lentamente hacia el norte a
nornoroeste.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 48 horas...baja...20 por ciento.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 7 días...baja...20 por ciento.
