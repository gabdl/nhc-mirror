For the central North Pacific...between 140W and 180W:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Hurricane
Polo, located less than 150 miles offshore of southwestern
Mexico, on Hurricane Odalys, located well west-southwest of the
southern tip of Baja California, and on recently upgraded Tropical
Storm Nolo, located a few hundred miles south of the Big Island of
Hawaii.

1. Well East-Southeast of the Hawaiian Islands:
An area of low pressure could form about 1000 miles east-southeast
of the Hawaiian Islands over the weekend. Environmental conditions
could support some gradual development of this system thereafter as
it moves generally northeastward, remaining well east-southeast of
the Hawaiian Islands.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...near 0 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...20 percent.

For the eastern and central North Pacific east of 180 longitude:

Active Systems:

of the Hawaiian Islands over the weekend. Environmental conditions 
could support some gradual development of this system thereafter as 
it moves generally northeastward, remaining well east-southeast of 
the Hawaiian Islands.

* Formation chance through 7 days...low...20 percent.

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
200 PM EDT Wed Sep 23 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

1. Far Eastern Tropical Atlantic (AL90):
An area of low pressure located a couple of hundred miles southwest 
off the coast of western Africa is gradually becoming better 
defined, and the associated shower and thunderstorm activity has 
increased since yesterday.  This system could become a short-lived 
tropical depression on Thursday or Friday while it moves 
northwestward to northward.  However, strong upper-level winds 
should end the opportunity for development by the weekend.  
Interests in the Cabo Verde Islands should monitor the progress of 
this system as gusty winds, areas of heavy rain and flash flooding 
is possible there by Friday.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...medium...50 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...medium...50 percent.

2. Central Subtropical Atlantic (Former Tropical Storm Fay):
Showers and thunderstorms have increased with the remnants of 
Fay located several hundred miles southwest of the Azores.  While 
environmental conditions are only marginally conducive for 
development, this system could become a tropical depression again 
today or tonight if the current thunderstorm activity persists.  
The system is forecast to move westward during the next couple of 
days, and by the weekend upper-level winds should inhibit formation 
chances.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...high...70 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...70 percent.

Para el Atlántico Norte...Mar Caribe y el Golfo de América

Lejano Oriente del Atlántico Tropical (AL90): Un área de baja
presión ubicada a un par de cientos de millas al suroeste de la
costa de África se está definiendo mejor gradualmente, y la
actividad de aguaceros y tormenta asociada ha aumentado desde ayer.
Este sistema podría convertirse en una depresión tropical de corta
duración el jueves o el viernes mientras se mueve del noroeste al
norte. Sin embargo, fuertes vientos de nivel superior deberían poner
fin a la oportunidad de desarrollo para el fin de semana. Los
interesados en las Islas de Cabo Verde deben monitorear el progreso
de este sistema, ya que es posible que se produzcan ráfagas de
viento, áreas de lluvia intensa e inundaciones repentinas allí para
el viernes.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 48 horas...medio...50 por ciento.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 7 días...media...50 por ciento.

Atlántico Subtropical Central (Ex Tormenta Tropical Fay): Los
aguaceros y las tormentas eléctricas han aumentado con los
remanentes de Fay ubicados a varios cientos de millas al suroeste de
las Azores. Si bien las condiciones ambientales solo son
marginalmente propicias para el desarrollo, este sistema podría
convertirse en una depresión tropical nuevamente hoy o esta noche si
persiste la actividad de tormenta actual. Se pronostica que el
sistema se desplazará hacia el oeste durante los próximos dos días,
y para el fin de semana los vientos de nivel superior deberían
inhibir las probabilidades de formación.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 48 horas...alta...70 por ciento.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 7 días...alta...70 por ciento.
