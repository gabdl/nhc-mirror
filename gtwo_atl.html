Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS Central Pacific Hurricane Center Honolulu HI
Issued by NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
800 PM HST Sun Sep 27 2026

For the central North Pacific...between 140W and 180W:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Hurricane
Polo, located about 155 miles southwest of Cabo San Lazaro, Mexico,
on Hurricane Nolo, located a few hundred miles southwest of the
Hawaiian Islands, and on Tropical Storm Rachel, located a few
hundred miles southwest of southern Mexico.

1. Well East-Southeast of the Hawaiian Islands (EP91):
A broad area of low pressure located well east-southeast of the
Hawaiian Islands continues to become better organized. Environmental
conditions are expected to be conducive for further development, and
a tropical depression is likely to form during the next day or so.
The system should move generally northeastward and remain well east
of the Hawaiian Islands.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...high...70 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...90 percent.



Public Advisories on Tropical Storm Rachel are issued under
WMO header WTPZ33 KNHC and under AWIPS header MIATCPEP3.
Forecast/Advisories on Tropical Storm Rachel are issued
under WMO header WTPZ23 KNHC and under AWIPS header MIATCMEP3.

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
200 AM EDT Mon Sep 28 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Tropical 
Depression Fay, located well to the southwest of the Azores.

1. Central Subtropical Atlantic (AL91):
A trough of low pressure located several hundred miles 
east-northeast of Bermuda is moving quickly east-northeastward. This 
system is producing disorganized showers and thunderstorms, and it 
could become a tropical depression over the next couple of days as 
it turns eastward to east-southeastward and slows down over the 
central Atlantic. Environmental conditions are expected to become 
unfavorable for further development later this week.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...medium...50 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...medium...60 percent.



Forecaster Evans/Mahoney

Para el Atlántico Norte...Mar Caribe y el Golfo de América

Sistemas activos: El Centro Nacional de Huracanes está emitiendo
advertencias sobre la Depresión Tropical Fay, ubicada al suroeste de
las Azores.

Atlántico Subtropical Central (AL91): Una vaguada de baja presión
ubicada a varios cientos de millas al este-noreste de las Bermudas
se está moviendo rápidamente hacia el este-noreste. Este sistema
está produciendo aguaceros y tormentas desorganizadas, y podría
convertirse en una depresión tropical en los próximos dos días a
medida que gira hacia el este-sureste y se ralentiza sobre el
Atlántico central. Se anticipa que las condiciones ambientales se
vuelvan desfavorables para un mayor desarrollo a finales de esta
semana.
* Probabilidad de formación hasta 48 horas...medio...50 por ciento.
* Probabilidad de formación en 7 días...mediana...60 por ciento.
