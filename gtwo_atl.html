Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
500 PM PDT Sat Oct 17 2026

For the eastern and central North Pacific east of 180 longitude:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Hurricane 
Trudy, located offshore of southwestern Mexico.

1. South of Southern Mexico:
An area of low pressure is forecast to form well offshore of 
southern Mexico within a couple of days. Environmental conditions 
appear conducive for gradual development thereafter, and a tropical 
depression is likely to form around the middle of next week while 
it moves westward to west-northwestward, well south of Mexico. 
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...near 0 percent. 
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...80 percent.

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
800 PM EDT Sat Oct 17 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

1. Off the East Coast of the United States:
A strong non-tropical low pressure area is expected to form by 
Sunday night off the southeast coast of the United States. This low 
is likely to move slowly and erratically through the middle of next 
week, and some subtropical development is possible during this 
time. Regardless of development, this system will cause high winds, 
coastal flooding, and dangerous surf along portions of the U.S. East 
Coast. Please consult statements and warnings from local National 
Weather Service offices and the Weather Prediction Center for more 
information, along with High Seas Forecasts issued by the National 
Weather Service.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...near 0 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...30 percent.



High Seas Forecasts issued by the National Weather Service
can be found under AWIPS header NFDHSFAT1, WMO header FZNT01 
KWBC, and online at ocean.weather.gov/shtml/NFDHSFAT1.php


Forecaster Blake
