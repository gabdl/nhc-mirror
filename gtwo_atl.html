ZCZC HFOTWOCP ALL
TTAA00 PHFO DDHHMM

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS Central Pacific Hurricane Center Honolulu HI
Issued by NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
200 PM HST Wed Nov 04 2026

For the central North Pacific...between 140W and 180W:

1. Well Southeast of the Hawaiian Islands (CP95):
Showers and thunderstorms associated with a trough of low
pressure located over the eastern portion of the Central Pacific
basin are gradually becoming better organized.  Environmental
conditions appear conducive for additional development of this
system, and a tropical depression is likely to form over the
Central Pacific basin during the next few days while it moves
westward to west-northwestward at 10 to 15 mph.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...medium...60 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...70 percent.



Forecaster Evans/Blake

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
400 PM PST Wed Nov 4 2026

For the eastern and central North Pacific east of 180 longitude:

1. Well Southeast of the Hawaiian Islands (CP95): 
Showers and thunderstorms associated with a trough of low 
pressure located over the eastern portion of the Central Pacific 
basin are gradually becoming better organized.  Environmental 
conditions appear conducive for additional development of this 
system, and a tropical depression is likely to form over the 
Central Pacific basin during the next few days while it moves 
westward to west-northwestward at 10 to 15 mph.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...medium...60 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...70 percent.

2. Eastern East Pacific:
A broad area of low pressure is producing a large area of 
disorganized showers and thunderstorms offshore of the coast of 
Central America. Gradual development of this system is possible 
during the next several days, and a tropical depression could form 
by this weekend while the low drifts northward. Regardless of 
development, this system could bring areas of heavy rain to portions 
of Central America through this weekend.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...medium...40 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...medium...60 percent.



Forecaster Evans/Blake

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
700 PM EST Wed Nov 4 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

1. Western and Central Atlantic:
A non-tropical low pressure area is located a few hundred miles 
south of Bermuda.  Environmental conditions could support this 
system gradually acquiring subtropical characteristics through this 
weekend while it moves slowly eastward over the western and central 
subtropical Atlantic.  By early next week, environmental conditions 
are forecast to become unfavorable for further development.  For 
more information on this system, including Gale Warnings, please see 
High Seas Forecasts issued by the National Weather Service. 
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...30 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...medium...50 percent.



High Seas Forecasts issued by the National Weather Service can be 
found under AWIPS header NFDHSFAT1, WMO header FZNT01 KWBC, and 
online at ocean.weather.gov/shtml/NFDHSFAT1.php


Forecaster Evans/Blake
