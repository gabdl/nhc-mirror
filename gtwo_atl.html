Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
500 PM PDT Sat Oct 10 2026

For the eastern and central North Pacific east of 180 longitude:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Tropical 
Storm Rachel, located several hundred miles south-southwest of Punta 
Baja, Mexico, and on Hurricane Simon, located less than one hundred 
miles south of Manzanillo, Mexico.

1. South of Southwestern Mexico:
An area of low pressure is forecast to develop south or southwest of 
the coast of Mexico by early next week. Environmental conditions are 
expected to be conducive for gradual development thereafter, and a 
tropical depression is likely to form by the middle or latter half 
of next week as the system moves generally west-northwestward at 
around 10 mph.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...near 0 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...70 percent.



Forecaster Adams

Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
200 AM EDT Sun Oct 11 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

Active Systems:
The Weather Prediction Center is issuing advisories on 
Post-Tropical Cyclone Isaias, located inland over Tennessee.

1. Eastern Tropical Atlantic:
An elongated area of low pressure centered about 100 miles 
southwest of the Cabo Verde Islands is producing a large but 
disorganized area of showers and thunderstorms extending from 
near those islands southward for several hundred miles.  Some 
development of this system is possible during the next couple of 
days while the low north-northwestward before upper-level winds 
become too strong for development by midweek.  Very heavy rainfall 
is also possible during the next few days over portions of the Cabo 
Verde Islands regardless of whether the system ever becomes a 
tropical cyclone.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...10 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...20 percent.



Forecaster Blake

Para el Atlántico Norte...Mar Caribe y el Golfo de América

Sistemas activos: El Centro de Predicción del Tiempo está emitiendo
advertencias sobre el Ciclón Pos-Tropical Isaias, ubicado tierra
adentro sobre Tennessee.

Atlántico Tropical Este: Un área alargada de baja presión centrada a
unas 100 millas al suroeste de las Islas Cabo Verde está produciendo
un área grande pero desorganizada de aguaceros y tormentas
eléctricas que se extienden desde cerca de esas islas hacia el sur
durante varios cientos de millas. Es posible que se desarrolle este
sistema durante los próximos dos días, mientras que los vientos
bajos del norte-noroeste antes de que los niveles altos se vuelvan
demasiado fuertes para que se desarrolle a mediados de semana.
También es posible que lluvias muy fuertes se produzcan durante los
próximos días sobre porciones de las Islas Cabo Verde,
independientemente de si el sistema se convierte en un ciclón
tropical.
